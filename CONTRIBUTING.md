# Contributing

Issues, questions and reports of what you saw in your own runs are welcome.

Code and documentation contributions can only be merged with a signed contributor licence agreement that lets the
author keep offering the work under other licences as well as the AGPL and CC BY-SA (dual licensing). Open an
issue first: the agreement is sent then.

The experiment has rules of its own (`brain/PLAN.md`, standing rules): nothing of the world in the brain's code or
prompts, no concept programmed, hormones modelled on their known human effects, no death. A change that breaks one
of them will not be merged, however good it is.
